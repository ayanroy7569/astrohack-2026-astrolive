The Problem
AstroLive converts users during emotional peaks (breakups, job stress, marriage doubts) but has no mechanism to bring them back afterward. It's a transactional consultation tool, not a daily habit — resulting in high one-time conversion but poor retention, and a revenue model capped by per-call pricing.
The Solution — Sankalp
Sankalp adds a new layer to AstroLive built around recurring rituals instead of one-time consultations:
🛕 Verified Puja Booking — book a real puja at a partner temple, personalized with devotee name, gotra, and a chosen occasion (birthday, Shraddh, festival, health & peace).
🎥 Video Proof — every puja is recorded and shared as proof the ritual was actually performed, solving the core trust problem in online religious services.
📦 Prasad Delivery — blessed prasad and ritual items shipped to the devotee's home.
🛍️ Astro Shop — a curated spiritual store (rudraksha, gemstones, puja kits, books) cross-sold at the moment of booking.
📅 Spiritual Calendar — remembers birthdays, Shraddh dates, and festivals, and nudges users to book — turning AstroLive into a habit rather than a crisis-only app.
✨ AstroLive Circle — a ₹299/month subscription for recurring puja reminders, priority booking, and discounts — a new, predictable revenue stream beyond per-call pricing.
How This Maps to the Brief
Judging Criteria	How Sankalp Delivers
Organic Growth	Shareable digital puja certificates after each ritual — natural, culturally authentic social sharing (like sharing a temple visit) that drives referral.
Habit-Forming	Spiritual Calendar + recurring puja subscriptions replace one-off crisis visits with regular, dated returns to the app.
New Revenue Model	AstroLive Circle subscription + puja bookings + spiritual store, on top of existing per-call consultation revenue.
USP	Video-proof verification + trust protection (payment held until proof is uploaded) — directly answers the biggest fear in online religious services: "did they actually do it?"
